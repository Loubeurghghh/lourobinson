Background

Engineering leader.
Builder of teams and things.

I'm an Engineering Manager based in West Moors, Dorset, with a track record of scaling engineering organisations through high-growth funding stages: Dexory (Series A to C), Sylvera (Seed to Series B), and InfoSum (Series B). I report directly to CTOs, own platform strategy and budget, and build the kind of teams where engineers grow into leaders themselves.

Before moving into software, I spent over eight years at Ageas Insurance across motor, household, and personal injury, which means I understand regulated, high-stakes environments from the inside. I started in QA, moved into DevOps and platform engineering, and have been leading engineering teams for the past several years.

I use Claude Code daily and build production AI products in my own time. I care deeply about engineering culture, psychological safety, and the discipline of doing things properly, which probably explains both the engineering career and the sludge metal.

Gatepost
Live

A B2B compliance research tool for UK food and agricultural businesses, built on a RAG architecture. Embeds regulatory documents via Voyage AI, retrieves semantically relevant chunks through pgvector cosine search, and generates cited, uncertainty-flagged answers using the Anthropic API. Includes a parallel compliance checklist generation pipeline per business profile. Live with a founding customer.
